# Demo Video Script — Prajwal Patil
# ~90 seconds. Plain and simple.

---

## BEFORE YOU START
- Open dashboard.html in Chrome. Make it fullscreen.
- Uvicorn is running in the background. Status dot is green.
- Clean desktop. No other tabs. Mic working.

---

## WHAT TO SAY AND DO

---

**[0:00 – 0:12] Face the camera. Say this:**

"When a company claims a hurricane delayed their shipment,
proving it takes 60 days and costs 50 lakh rupees in legal fees.
I built a tool that does it in under 3 seconds.
Watch."

---

**[0:12 – 0:22] Turn to screen. Click the Hurricane preset button.**

Say:

"I'm typing a real force majeure claim — the kind a lawyer would write.
I'll tell it where the storm was and when."

---

**[0:22 – 0:50] Click Adjudicate. While it loads, say:**

"No special format. No forms to fill. Just plain English."

**When the result appears, point at the screen and read it out:**

"Validated. Peak wind — 51.6 metres per second. That's a Category 3 hurricane.
24 hours straight above the legal threshold.
Data comes from the US government's weather records —
the same ones courts accept as evidence.
Time taken — under 3 seconds."

---

**[0:50 – 1:05] Click the Invalid Claim preset. Hit Adjudicate. Say:**

"Now watch what happens with a fake claim."

**When Rejected appears:**

"Rejected instantly. 2 milliseconds.
It didn't even look at the database.
No analyst wasted. No cost incurred."

---

**[1:05 – 1:20] Face the camera again. Say:**

"60 days. 50 lakh rupees. Replaced by one button.

The data behind this is 1.46 billion real weather observations
from 15,000 stations around the world.

If your business deals with weather-related disputes —
shipping, insurance, construction, aviation —
I'm offering a 30-day paid pilot.

The system is ready. Today."

*Pause one second on "Today." Then stop recording.*
